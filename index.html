Energy-Based Multi-Latent Variable Generative Modeling for Dynamic Patterns

Defu Yang, Shaowen Lu, Jing Yan


Abstract

This paper proposes an energy-based generative video modeling framework with hierarchical latent variables for learning complex spatiotemporal dynamics. Conventional latent variable models typically rely on fixed Gaussian priors, whose limited expressive capacity poses significant challenges in modeling complex dynamic patterns in videos and time-series data. To overcome the representational limitations of fixed Gaussian priors, we introduce layer-wise energy functions for each latent variable, enabling the capture of intra-layer contextual structures while jointly modeling and correcting the dependencies across different layers of latent variables. In addition, we employ shortrun MCMC as the inference engine for latent variables, using a finite number of Langevin dynamics steps to approximate the posterior distribution, thereby avoiding the need for separate inference networks such as variational encoders. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method significantly outperforms variational autoencoder (VAE)-based generative models in terms of generation quality and temporal consistency.
